USFS · National Forest System Trails

The Forest Service's authoritative trail inventory, published from its Enterprise Data Warehouse. The attribute-richest federal source.

Scope
USFS-managed lands, CONUS + Alaska. 86,073 segments, ~160,000 miles.
License
US Government work, public domain. No use restrictions; courtesy attribution to USDA Forest Service.
Access
ArcGIS REST query API, GeoJSON, no key. 2,000 features per request. Bulk download as FileGDB / Shapefile.
Updated
Rolling, forest by forest. National extract refreshed 2026-06-28.
Attributes
Federal Trail Data Standards: trail class TC1–5, surface, typical grade, tread width, and per-use management status incl. bicycle and e-bike classes 1–3.
+ Authoritative geometry and management attributes
+ Cleanest license of any source
− Forest Service land only; attribute depth varies by forest
− No live conditions or closures in this layer
